---
title: Goblins of the Hill Country
tags: [goblin]
---
Goblins in these hills live in small bands that raid farms and travellers. They
are cowardly alone and cruel in numbers, and they fear their bigger hobgoblin
cousins. A goblin set to guard something guards it badly: it naps, gambles with
bones and keeps a fire going against the damp. They speak Goblin and a broken
Common, and they respect strength and shiny things more than promises. Their
blades are crude, notched and often stolen.
