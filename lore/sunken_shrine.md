---
title: The Sunken Shrine
tags: [shrine]
---
Long ago the villagers of the valley built a small shrine to the dawn into the
hillside, so that the first light came down the stairs at midsummer. The sun is
carved on its doors and its locks. When the spring beneath it broke through the
floor, the priests left and the shrine slowly filled with water. Villagers say
the keepers left something precious on the altar when they went, and that the
shrine has drawn thieves and worse ever since.
